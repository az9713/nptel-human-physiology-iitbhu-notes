# House style

- Developed textbook prose for an advanced quantitative reader new to biology. One paragraph develops one scientific idea; define terminology before use.
- Preserve lecture topic order as the coverage spine, with timestamps beside headings. Keep production narration out of the scientific body.
- A recurring case motivates each chapter and returns after the mechanism/model is established. An empirical mechanism needs source support; mathematical relations need stated assumptions and derivations where appropriate.
- Distinguish lecture coverage, source corrections, verified research extensions, and original teaching models. Date current clinical/regulatory claims.
- Standard anatomical position and explicit orientation; name compartments and membrane sides. Specify species/specimen/state and scale where relevant.
- SI units by default, with physiological conventions (mmHg, mL/dL, mmol/L) defined and conversions explicit. No mixing concentration, content, flow, flux and delivery.
- Introduce each symbol locally and maintain plan/notation.md. Scope repeated symbols by chapter when unavoidable. No decorative equations.
- Dark-first shared reader CSS/JS, readable serif prose, local MathJax, numbered figures/equations, accessible controls, and print stylesheet.
- Dynamic processes: animation-first, pause/replay/speed/seek or phase stepping as appropriate; synchronize plots and anatomy from one biological state. Label artificial colors, geometric exaggeration and timescale changes.
- Photorealistic anatomy from referenced geometry and calibrated materials; schematic mechanisms declare their abstraction. Anatomical realism and scientific mechanism are checked separately.
- Code follows the explained model, has reproducible output, and states input/output, units, simplifying assumptions and failure boundaries. Prose is authored directly in HTML.
- Six solved problems is the reference default; vary conceptual, derivational and computational roles to suit the biology. Do not force theorem-style proofs on empirical claims.
- Main prose integrates citations beside supported claims. A source appendix records provenance/access limits and the sources actually read.
- Shared book shell follows the Nano Photonics reference assets; remove course-specific rules when encountered and test at desktop, narrow/mobile and print sizes.
