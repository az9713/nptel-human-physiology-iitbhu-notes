# Book specification

## Working title and source
Human Physiology — Independent Study Notes. Source course: NPTEL102106670, Prof. Sudip Mukherjee, IIT (BHU) Varanasi. The supplied upload channel is not the teaching institution.

## Conceptual spine
Explain how compartmentalized cells and coordinated organ systems maintain a viable internal environment through transport, signaling, exchange, mechanical work and feedback. Follow the source course's substantive order while making each chapter readable independently.

## Reader and prerequisites
A quantitatively trained graduate new to physiology. Assume algebra, elementary calculus, basic chemistry and willingness to learn biological terminology. Define cellular, anatomical and physiological vocabulary locally. Explain new differential-equation and control ideas when used; no assumed clinical training.

## Reader before and after
Before: can manipulate equations but lacks a coherent anatomical and physiological model.
After: can locate major structures; explain the mechanisms in all60 official lectures; distinguish content from flow, signal from transported substance, empirical claim from model, normal variation from pathology, and approved practice from investigational work; reproduce selected quantitative examples and interrogate animations.

Chapter outcomes map to result nodes in plan/results.yaml and worked exercises. Empirical biological claims require evidence, not invented proofs.

## Scope
All60 official lectures, with the short course introduction supplementary.25 conceptually grouped chapters;73 planned dynamic explanations and35 planned anatomical/comparison illustrations, subject to review of teaching purpose. No visual-count quota. About29.327 source hours. Each chapter receives the developed prose, worked examples, sources, reproducible computation where meaningful and complete solutions appropriate to its scope.

## Exclusions
Personal diagnosis, individualized prescribing, treatment algorithms claimed safe for real patients, unsupported clinical extrapolation, and presentation of schematic dynamics as experimental evidence. Material beyond the lecture spine is labeled as a research extension or original teaching construction.

## Format and numbering
Root chapter-NN-slug.html with matching meta.json; dark responsive HTML + locally bundled MathJax. Chapter.N numbering; clickable timestamps and cross-references; shared navigation, glossary, source map, references and code labs. Animation is primary for temporal mechanisms; include controls and accessible/print alternatives.

## Evidence and review
Use raw captions and sampled video evidence privately. Correct terminology and mechanisms against authoritative sources with traceable claim provenance. Research clinical claims against current owning sources. Two independent substantive reviews per chapter; targeted checks following repairs. Scientific and visual checks are distinct.

Release after acceptance and actual browser/code/source checks; verify remote and Pages output. Raw sources, work records and local paths remain private.
