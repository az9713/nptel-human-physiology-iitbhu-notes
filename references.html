Claim-level references

References accompany the chapter claims they support. The chapters distinguish lecture statements, researched corrections, original teaching constructions and source-access limits. Consult each chapter's evidence section and current review status; a linked source alone does not establish that every claim has been verified.

Evidence policy

Separate lecture statements, independently researched corrections and original teaching constructions. Caption hashes are recorded in the source manifest; those hashes establish identity, not correctness. Review acceptance requires two independent full scientific reviews bound to the current manuscript and its assets. A static development check cannot replace scientific or browser review.

Visual sources and licenses

Photographs and external body geometry retain their original creator, accession, license and modification records in the asset attribution register. Modality colors, specimen differences and calibration limits are stated beside the figures.

Offline reader components

Navigation, search and reader controls use local files. External source links need network access when followed. Mathematical typesetting uses the locally bundled MathJax distribution; see its license.
