Chapter 1 · Marker-defined cell structures

Peroxisomes and cytoskeleton

Two independent microscopy studies reveal selected cellular structures through fluorescent labels. The colors report labelling; these images do not form a registered map of one cell.

Separate specimens: the fibroblast and HeLa fields are not registered to each other or to the chapter’s other cell images. These selected marker observations do not complete a whole-cell inventory or demonstrate organelle trafficking.
