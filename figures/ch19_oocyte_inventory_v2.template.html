Only the oocyte / maternal product is counted. Somatic cells, polar bodies and paternal genome are excluded. This is not a lineage-DNA conservation plot or a zygote inventory. After meiosis II, the 23 single chromatids are now daughter chromosomes. C denotes conventional haploid DNA content; n denotes chromosome-set status.

The first five follicle states retain the same inventory despite differing tissue morphology. State 7 is conditional. Neither it nor an individual human cell’s full history is measured in the mouse image. Intermediate anaphase counts, developmental fate and equal stage durations are outside this model.
