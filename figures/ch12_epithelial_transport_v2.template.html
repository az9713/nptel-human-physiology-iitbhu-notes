Fructose uses the same three-sugar order through GLUT5 entry and GLUT2 exit, with zero directly coupled Na⁺. This does not mean a real cell contains no sodium. Other sodium turnover is outside the ledger.

  1. Apical entry: SGLT1 couples two Na⁺ to one glucose/galactose under a favourable Na⁺ electrochemical driving condition. GLUT5 facilitates fructose entry without directly coupled Na⁺ or ATP.
  2. Basolateral exit: GLUT2 facilitates sugar exit when the sugar gradient favours it. It is not an ATP-driven outlet or a one-way valve.
  3. Separate concurrent maintenance: basolateral Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase exports three Na⁺ and imports two K⁺ per ATP, helping maintain the Na⁺ gradient. No synchronized pump cycles or finite ATP pool are imposed here.

Interstitium is not blood or lymph. Intestinal capillaries receive these sugars for portal delivery; separately packaged long-chain lipids use chylomicron and lacteal routes. The ledger does not model those vessels, sugar metabolism, water movement, membrane voltage or transporter regulation.
