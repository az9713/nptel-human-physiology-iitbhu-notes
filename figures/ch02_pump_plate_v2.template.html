The matched camera connects the subunit envelopes with their deposited Cα traces. Colors identify chains; this is a molecular reconstruction, not a visible-light photograph.

What this structure establishes

The deposited state is Na-bound and precedes E1P: an ADP/AlF₄ phosphorylation-transition analogue, without oligomycin. These protein-only views omit the deposited ions, nucleotide, glycans, lipids and waters. One structure supplies no pump cycle, ion trajectory or kinetic rate.
