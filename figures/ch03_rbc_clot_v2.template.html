Chapter 3 · Independent microscopy specimens

Red-cell shape and fibrin redistribution

A prepared human erythrocyte SEM supplies surface-shape evidence. A separate live human platelet-rich-plasma assay records fluorescent fibrin redistribution during clot retraction. The specimens, preparation and signals differ; they do not supply a complete normal-human blood-cell differential or a physiological thrombosis sequence.
