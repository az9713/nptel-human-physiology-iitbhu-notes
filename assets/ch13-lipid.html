Abstract compartments; gold cargo and ring sizes are symbolic. Neither a mixed aggregate nor a secretory vesicle travels intact from lumen into a lacteal.

Static sequence and evidence boundary

Emulsification changes droplet size; lipase/colipase supports TAG → fatty acids + MAG. Luminal mixed aggregates solubilize products. Individual cargo uptake precedes ER re-esterification and apoB-48/MTP-associated chylomicron assembly, then Golgi/secretory trafficking. A monolayer chylomicron travels within a bilayer secretory vesicle.

The vesicle fuses at the enterocyte basolateral membrane, releasing chylomicron into interstitium. The released particle then reaches a separate lacteal → intestinal lymph → thoracic duct → systemic venous blood. Shorter-chain fatty acids can favor a portal route after epithelial transfer; this is not an absolute size-only rule.

The basolateral opening symbolizes a fusion site, not a permanently open hole in a real cell. Lacteal opening is a route abstraction, not a calibrated junction. Original deterministic staged mechanism; no realistic anatomy or measured motion is claimed. Chapter explanation and sources.
