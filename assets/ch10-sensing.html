Blood, tissue and processed sensor

Forced reservoir: blood changes at 10 and 40 min. Tissue lag 5 min; processing lag 2 min. All values and times are synthetic.

Starts paused, including reduced motion. At 1×, one second of playback advances five model minutes. Seeking selects a deterministic state. Focus the diagram and use arrow keys to pan its wide view.

Static reading and model limits

Blood changes first, interstitial glucose relaxes next, and the processed sensor follows. Blood is maintained externally; this is not a whole-body amount model. No geometry is measured anatomy. Particle symbols, where shown, are species keys rather than molecule counts. Time is an explicit synthetic model clock and animation speed has no effect on its equations.
