This register covers the current assets for Chapters1–25. Linked local records preserve exact image/geometry identities, acquisition evidence, hashes, modifications and limits. Third-party assets retain their stated licenses; a source's license is not extended to unrelated chapter material. Original chapter schematics, code and controlled animations are separate constructions by the chapter authors, not copied lecture artwork or experimental movies. Their references are in the chapter captions and linked records. No new license grant is asserted here for original material.
