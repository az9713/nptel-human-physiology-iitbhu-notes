Static sequence and evidence boundary

Acid unfolds; pepsin and pancreatic proteases cleave. Enteropeptidase initiates trypsin activation. Brush-border aminopeptidases act luminally; PEPT1 imports di/tripeptide with H+; intracellular peptidases release amino acids. Basolateral exit → interstitial fluid → intestinal capillary → portal vein/liver.

Apical NHE3 imports Na+ and exports H+. Basolateral Na+/K+-ATPase exports 3 Na+ and imports 2 K+ per ATP. These directions are maintenance relationships, not a synchronized ion-count simulation. Free amino acids also have separate apical uptake systems.
