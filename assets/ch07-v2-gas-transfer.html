The same mechanism as a still sequence

  1. Compare air and blood partial pressures separately for each gas.
  2. A positive air-minus-blood difference gives net transfer toward blood; a negative difference gives net transfer toward air.
  3. For the air-to-blood route, the source-labelled spatial order is Alv → AEC1 → shared basal lamina (*) → Endo → Cap. The reverse route traverses the same barrier in the opposite direction.
  4. Both gas examples progress together. The fixed image, oversized symbols and eight illustration seconds supply no measured molecular kinetics or molar flux.
  5. Equal pressures remove the net-transfer symbol; microscopic exchange does not cease.
