Static / print alternative. HPA: hypothalamic CRH promotes anterior-pituitary ACTH, which promotes adrenal cortical cortisol. Cortisol restrains upstream drive. Calcium: low ionized calcium stimulates PTH; bone release and renal conservation support the extracellular pool, while renal calcitriol supports intestinal absorption. Rising calcium restrains PTH. Bone is an internal reservoir; gut absorption and urinary loss cross the body boundary.
