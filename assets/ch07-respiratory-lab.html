Scale labels change explicitly. These panels are connected in function, not drawn to a common anatomical scale.

The presentation highlights upstream clearance then gas transfer. Its 12-second duration and marker speeds are chosen for readability; no capillary transit time, molecule velocity or measured equilibration fraction is represented. Gas permeates intact membranes and liquid; the barrier bands are not openings for red cells.

S(p) = 1 / [1 + (P₅₀/p)ⁿ], S(0)=0. Selected illustrative parameters; no fitted data. Each heme symbol has the same fractional opacity to represent expected occupancy. This is an equilibrium curve, not simulated binding/unbinding kinetics. Raising P₅₀ lowers affinity at fixed p; delivery additionally depends on hemoglobin content and flow.
