Organization across scales

Follow the question from the whole body to an organ, a tissue, and a cell. Each stop changes what must be explained.

A 16-second presentation sequence, not biological time or development. All shapes and scale windows are illustrative. The four drawings are separate schematics; no continuous microscope zoom, registered specimen, or measured body dimensions are implied.

1. Body / organism

Chosen scale window: 1 m

Organ systems cooperate in the living individual. Skin is considered next as one organ.

2. Organ / skin

Chosen scale window: 10 cm

An organ combines tissue types. A block symbol exposes their arrangement; its proportions are invented.

3. Tissue / matrix

Chosen scale window: 1 mm

Cell relationships and the surrounding matrix matter. A tissue is more than isolated cells placed together.

4. Cell / compartments

Chosen scale window: 10 μm

Selective boundaries and organelles organize transport. This is a generic cell, not the measured fibroblast below.
