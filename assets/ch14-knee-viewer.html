Rigid-bone replay

Transform and native reference points

For every bone vertex: x(t) = c(t) + R(q(t)) × (x0 − c0). Quaternion order is x,y,z,w; R is active reference-to-current rotation. The reference/pivot called center_of_mass in the input is not a measured anatomical centroid. No mesh is individually recentered. Tibia and fibula remain fixed. Reported JCS angles are not world Euler angles; reported moment is a scalar with unverified world-vector convention.
