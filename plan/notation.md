# Shared notation and terminology

Each symbol also receives a local definition before substantive use. This initial table expands as source-grounded sections are drafted; chapter-local exceptions must be explicit.

| Symbol/term | Meaning | Units/domain | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| t | Physical time; distinguish animation presentation clock | s or explicitly stated biological unit | book |
| c_i | Concentration of species i in a named compartment | mol/m³, mmol/L, or explicitly stated convention | book |
| V_comp | Volume of a named compartment | m³ or L | book |
| J_i | Flux density of species i | mol/(m² s), or stated species convention | transport |
| Q_flow | Volumetric flow rate | m³/s or mL/min | circulation/renal |
| P | Pressure with named compartment/subscript | Pa or mmHg | book |
| V_m | Membrane potential, inside relative to outside | mV | transport/neuroscience |
| D_i | Diffusion coefficient of species i | m²/s | transport |
| R_flow | Hydraulic resistance | pressure/volumetric-flow units | circulation |
| HR | Heart rate | beats/min | cardiac |
| SV | Stroke volume | mL/beat | cardiac |
| CO | Cardiac output; spell out carbon monoxide when relevant | L/min | cardiac; disambiguate respiratory text |
| C_aO2 | Arterial oxygen content | mL O2/dL blood | respiratory |
| S_aO2 | Arterial hemoglobin oxygen saturation | dimensionless fraction, not a percentage inside equations | respiratory |
| GFR | Glomerular filtration rate | mL/min | renal |
| RPF | Renal plasma flow | mL/min | renal |
| filtration fraction | GFR/RPF | dimensionless | renal |
| apical/basolateral | Epithelial membrane side relative to lumen and interstitium | anatomical convention | book |
| afferent/efferent | Toward/away from a named reference structure | anatomical convention | book |

Keep tonicity distinct from osmolality, affinity from content, content from delivery, secretion from reabsorption, and oocyte stage from follicular stage.
