Reviewed study edition. Each chapter record identifies its current status. Manuscript availability, source acquisition, scientific review and actual reader verification are distinct. Source-video audits use bounded sampling and do not claim continuous viewing.

Learning route

Follow the numbered chapters. The progression connects cells and homeostasis to transport, signaling and integrated organ-system physiology. Chapter records below identify prerequisites by chapter number. Quantitative models will state their assumptions, units and limits.
